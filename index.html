Calculation Information

This calculator uses linear elastic Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.

The basic bending stress relationship is:

σ = M × c / I = M / Z

where:

  • M = bending moment
  • I = second moment of area
  • c = distance from neutral axis
  • Z = section modulus

The calculation assumes small deflection, linear elastic material behavior, constant cross section and idealized supports.
